# Rexi's Revenge

A comedic pixel-art arena shooter in the style of Heli Attack 3. Rexi, a very muscular judge, survives endless waves of flying enemies sent by crooked lawyers allied with a rival gym. The game is shown to the player in Spanish.

## Language

**Rexi**:
The player character: a very muscular judge with light-brown hair, a judge's robe with the sleeves torn off, and a tattoo of a lion head next to a classical columned courthouse on his left arm.
_Avoid_: Player, hero, protagonist

**Arena**:
The single screen where a Run takes place: the plaza between the courthouse and the Bufete & Pesas S.A. tower. Rexi stays in it and Enemies fly into it.
_Avoid_: Level, stage, map

**Run**:
One endless survival attempt, from spawning until Rexi is defeated, scored by points.
_Avoid_: Game, match, round

**Bufete & Pesas S.A.**:
The antagonist faction: crooked lawyers Rexi once sentenced, allied with a rival gym. Every enemy is sent by them.
_Avoid_: The Syndicate, villains, bad guys

**Enemy**:
A flying machine sent by Bufete & Pesas S.A. that Rexi destroys (this game's version of Heli Attack's helicopters). Every Enemy is either Lawyer Craft or Gym Craft. In the Spanish UI, destroyed Enemies are counted as "demandas desestimadas".
_Avoid_: Helicopter, craft, nave, demanda (outside UI copy)

**Lawyer Craft**:
The light Enemies, flown by the crooked-lawyer side. In v1: Maletín-cóptero and Archivador Artillado.
_Avoid_: Light enemy

**Gym Craft**:
The heavy Enemies, flown by the rival-gym side. In v1: Caminadora a Reacción and Banca Artillada.
_Avoid_: Heavy enemy

**Quip**:
A line Rexi says, in Spanish, when he destroys an Enemy. Destroying Lawyer Craft draws a legal Quip and destroying Gym Craft draws a gym Quip.
_Avoid_: Phrase, line, catchphrase, frase

**Dialogue Box**:
A Pokemon-style text box at the bottom of the screen that shows a Quip with typewriter text. It never blocks play.
_Avoid_: Speech bubble, popup

**Hit-stop**:
The short freeze-frame when a Quip triggers, just before the Dialogue Box appears.
_Avoid_: Pause, slow-mo

**Crate**:
A box that drops into the Arena carrying either a Weapon (ammo for it) or a Power-up.
_Avoid_: Pickup, drop, loot

**Weapon**:
A themed gun Rexi fires. Once collected, a Weapon stays in his inventory with its own ammo. Mazo Automático has unlimited ammo. The v1 Weapons are Mazo Automático, Lluvia de Sellos, Mancuernas, Código Penal, Citaciones Teledirigidas and Sentencia Firme.
_Avoid_: Gun

**Power-up**:
A temporary effect Rexi picks up. The v1 Power-ups are Pre-entreno (slow time), Receso (health), Inmunidad Judicial (invulnerability), Día de Pierna (jetpack) and Creatina (triple damage).
_Avoid_: Buff, item
